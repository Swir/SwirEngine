## PBR / glTF material fidelity

- glTF 2.0 material loading now preserves `normalTexture`, `occlusionTexture`, `emissiveTexture`, `emissiveFactor`, normal scale and occlusion strength alongside the existing base-color and metallic/roughness channels.
- The 3D PBR shader now evaluates tangent-space normal maps without requiring precomputed tangent vertex data, using a derivative-based TBN frame with a safe fallback for degenerate UVs.
- Occlusion maps modulate the ambient/indirect contribution, while emissive maps and factors contribute self-illumination independently from scene lights.
- PBR base-color and emissive textures are decoded from sRGB into linear space for lighting and encoded back to sRGB for output; metallic/roughness, occlusion and normal data remain sampled as linear data channels.
- Renderer live-asset tracking now watches every supported material texture channel so normal, occlusion and emissive edits invalidate the same GPU texture cache as base-color and metallic/roughness edits.
- Existing Phong materials retain their legacy shading/output path and existing public `Material3D` construction remains backwards compatible.
